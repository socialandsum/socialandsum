The mechanism

Four layers, working as one system.

Not "we run your ads." Social+Sum is acquisition, infrastructure and sales process, built to work together.

Creative

You provide your branding and a selection of images, we create the ad assets: on-brand, and built to perform, not just look good.

Acquisition

Meta campaigns with qualification built into the lead form itself, filtering for fit before a single enquiry reaches you.

Infrastructure

A CRM that responds the moment a lead comes in, with automated follow-up, reminders and reactivation, so nothing goes cold waiting on you.

Sales process

Qualification frameworks and consultation scripts, drawn from five years running sales funnels, so the leads that come in actually convert.

The guarantee

Guaranteed on what we control. Named honestly on what we don't.

Your cost per qualified opportunity is capped from day one, agreed in writing before you sign. If it ever runs above that ceiling, we keep optimising the campaigns at no extra cost to you until it's back under the cap, provided leads are followed up within the agreed window on your end. You never pay more than the agreed rate for a qualified opportunity.

We don't guarantee closed sales, because that comes down to your consultation and pricing, not our ad account. We say so plainly before you sign, not after, so there are no surprises later.
