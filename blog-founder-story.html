Founder story

Why I started Social + Sum, and why I want to help you

Luwana Bryan, Founder · 4 min read

People usually ask me some version of the same question: why marketing, and why interior design studios specifically? It's a fair question, and the honest answer starts with something I kept noticing rather than a single lightbulb moment.

The gap I kept seeing

My background is in B2B sales funnels and marketing, built over five years of watching how businesses actually turn interest into revenue, not just how they talk about it. Across that time, one pattern kept repeating: the businesses with the best product in the room were rarely the ones with the strongest pipeline. Skill and demand don't automatically connect to each other. Something has to build that bridge on purpose.

Interior design studios are one of the clearest versions of this I've come across. The work is genuinely beautiful, the craft is real, and the portfolios prove it. But a stunning portfolio sitting quietly on Instagram isn't a sales funnel, and most studios were never taught to think of it as one, because nobody trained them to. They were trained to design rooms, not to run acquisition systems. That's not a criticism, it's just a different discipline, and it's the one I happen to know.

Why I wanted to build this for studios, specifically

I wanted to work with people who care about the outcome as much as I do, and design studios tend to care deeply, about their clients, their reputation, and the quality of what they put their name on. That makes them a genuinely rewarding group to build for. My part isn't the design. It's everything that happens before someone becomes a client: getting the right people to notice the work, giving them an obvious next step, and following up before the moment passes.

  • I like fixing structural problems, not just running ads. A funnel that leaks at the enquiry stage doesn't get fixed by spending more on traffic into it.
  • I like being judged on the pipeline, not the vanity numbers. Followers and likes are easy to report and easy to hide behind. Booked calls and signed clients are the number that actually matters, and it's the one I hold myself to.
  • I like working with people who are already good at their craft. My job gets to be adding the missing half, not fixing the work itself.
The short version: I built Social + Sum because I kept watching talented studios stay busier with word of mouth than they should have needed to be, and because closing that gap is the part of the work I'm genuinely built for.

If any of this sounds like your studio, that's exactly who I set out to help, and I'd genuinely like to hear where your pipeline is leaking.

Luwana Bryan

Founder, Social + Sum
